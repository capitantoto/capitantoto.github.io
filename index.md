# Gonzalo Barrera Borla

Math nerd. Teacher by heart, engineer by trade. Buenos Aires (remote-friendly).

Statistician / ML engineer. My models have priced programmatic ad auctions
(Jampp), handicapped horse races, flagged crypto-wallet fraud (Muun), and
matched people looking for love (Sitch). The common thread: putting honest
probabilities on messy human behavior, then writing production code around
them.

MSc in Mathematical Statistics (FCEN-UBA) · BA in Economics (FCE-UBA) ·
statistics TA at the Instituto de Cálculo, because explaining something is
the only way I trust that I understand it.

**Open to remote DS / MLE / quant roles.**
